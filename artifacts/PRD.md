# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## K.A.R.S.H Athlete Coaching

### 1. Objective
To build a comprehensive platform for athlete coaching, enabling coaches to manage training programs, track athlete progress, and facilitate communication.

### 2. Target Audience
- **Athletes:** Looking for structured training, performance tracking, and direct feedback.
- **Coaches:** Needing a centralized tool to manage multiple athletes, design programs, and analyze data.

### 3. Key Features
- **User Authentication:** Secure login for athletes and coaches (Role-based access).
- **Dashboard:** Overview of upcoming workouts, recent achievements, and key metrics.
- **Workout Builder (Coaches):** Interface to create and assign daily/weekly training plans.
- **Activity Log (Athletes):** Interface to log completed workouts, upload metrics, and add notes.
- **Progress Tracking:** Charts and analytics on performance metrics over time.
- **Messaging/Feedback:** In-app communication between coach and athlete.

### 4. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Responsive Design:** Mobile-first approach preferred, as athletes often log data on the go.
- **Performance:** Fast load times and offline capabilities for logging workouts.
- **Security:** Secure data handling and privacy for health and performance data.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure the app is usable by people with disabilities (WCAG compliance).
